Core Competencies

Practice Disciplines

Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)

We deliver end-to-end management of proprietary assets, from registration and commercial portfolio audits to IP transactions. Our team safeguards creative and industrial assets against unauthorized exploitation across domestic and global markets.

Copyright, Trademark & Infringement Litigation

We secure prompt injunctive relief and damages against brand counterfeiting, piracy, trademark dilution, and passing-off violations. Our advocates handle opposition hearings, rectification proceedings, and aggressive trial enforcement to defend market identity.

Civil Matters & Property Disputes

We represent individuals and enterprises in suits for specific performance, injunctions, partition, title verification, and asset recovery. Our practice ensures strategic dispute resolution across trial courts, appellate benches, and specialized civil tribunals.

Criminal Defense & White-Collar Matters

We provide counsel for trial defense, regular and anticipatory bail, economic offenses, and statutory investigations. From FIR quashing to appellate advocacy, we safeguard client liberties with meticulous procedural rigor.

Matrimonial & Family Law

We handle matrimonial disputes with discretion, including contested divorce, mutual consent proceedings, child custody, and maintenance claims. Our counsel balances empathetic advisory with firm legal representation under applicable personal and domestic violence laws.

Writ Petitions & Constitutional Remedies, PIL-Supreme Court

We invoke the extraordinary writ jurisdictions of the High Courts and the Supreme Court under Articles 226 and 32 to challenge arbitrary state actions. Our team secures immediate judicial review, status-quo orders, and the enforcement of fundamental and statutory rights.

Commercial & Contractual Disputes

We resolve breach of contract actions, vendor disputes, partnership conflicts, and business arbitrations. Our focus is on mitigating commercial interruption while securing enforceable financial and equitable remedies.

Banking & Debt Recovery (NI Act / Section 138)

We prosecute and defend cheque bounce complaints, summary recovery suits, and proceedings before Debt Recovery Tribunals. We execute targeted legal recovery strategies designed to expedite financial settlements.

Consumer Protection & Real Estate (RERA)

We represent homebuyers and commercial consumers before RERA authorities, state commissions, and the NCDRC. Our advocates pursue accountability, compensation, and delayed possession remedies against builder-developer defaults.
